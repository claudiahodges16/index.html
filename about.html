Our Story

North Star Bakery was founded with a passion for baking and a commitment to quality. Our journey began in a small kitchen, where we created baked goods that delight the senses. Over the years, we have grown into a bakery that serves our community with freshly baked breads, pastries, and cakes. We are passionate about baking and bringing people together through fresh and locally sourced grains.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve our community with freshly baked goods that bring people together and make special occasions even more memorable.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baked goods start with quality ingredients. We use fresh ingredients and locally sourced grains from our trusted community partners. We carefully select our ingredients to create baked goods with delicious flavor and consistent quality.

Head Baker

Our Bakery Team

Our dedicated team works together to prepare fresh baked goods for our customers. They take pride in creating delicious treats that bring joy to our community.
